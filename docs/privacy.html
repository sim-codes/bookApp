Spinematch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Spinematch is a reading app for books you already have on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: everything stays on your device.

Information we collect

Spinematch does not collect, store or transmit any personal information to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Copies of the book files (EPUB, PDF and TXT) you choose to add.
  • Book details such as title, author and genre, and cover images, whether read from the book file, generated by the app, or picked by you.
  • Your reading progress, reading sessions, streaks and reading time.

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Delete all books & data, or by deleting individual books. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Permissions

  • Files: books are added through your device's system file picker. The app only receives the files you select.
  • Photos: if you choose a custom cover, it is picked through your device's system photo picker. The app only receives the image you select and does not access the rest of your photo library.

Spinematch does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

Spinematch does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If future versions add features that handle personal information, such as accounts or sharing, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/sim-codes/bookApp/issues.
